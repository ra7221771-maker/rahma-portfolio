Egypt Post — Visual Identity & Rebranding

Project Grade: A+

THE CONCEPT

Point Zero — Connecting Generations

“Point Zero” takes its name from the distance-measuring reference point at the Egyptian Postal Museum. The project reinterprets this landmark as a symbol of Egypt Post’s enduring role as a starting point for communication across generations.

From letters and stamps to financial services, Egypt Post has continually evolved to meet the needs of each era. Through a renewed visual identity, posters, and films, the project brings this adaptability to life, presenting its heritage and services in a contemporary way that connects with today’s generation.
