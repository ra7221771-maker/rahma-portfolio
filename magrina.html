01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

With Magrina, I wasn’t just creating content. I was building the content direction from the ground up. My role covered strategy, ideas, scripts, reels, paid ad creatives, and execution, with one clear goal: make content build trust, create connection, and turn attention into growth and sales.

02 / CHALLENGE

The Challenge

The brand started without a clear content direction. The challenge was to build a system that could sell through both organic content and paid ads while creating long-term trust between the audience and the brand.

03 / STRATEGY

The Strategy

I split the content between the founder’s personal account and the business account. The founder built trust through useful fashion education, while the brand mixed educational, relatable, and sales content across the funnel. Real people and different body types made the content feel more believable and human.

CREATIVE EXECUTION

Content designed to be watched, saved and acted on.

Different formats, different goals. Each piece of content was built around a specific audience need, product angle, or stage of the customer journey.
